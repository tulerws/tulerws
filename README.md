**I build quiet, local-first tools for working with AI agents.**

I'm a full-stack developer from Rio de Janeiro with a background in .NET and Angular, web and mobile. These days most of my time goes into Lume, an open-source desktop app that keeps Codex, Claude Code, Gemini and other agents in one place, without giving up your terminal or your history.

### Lume

<a href="https://lumeagents.dev"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lume-agents/Lume/main/docs/screenshots/lume-workspace.png" width="520" alt="The Lume Workspace: agents in the sidebar, two chats side by side and the session inspector" /></a>

A floating orb that shows what every agent is doing, and a workspace to review their work, approve what they ask and keep going. Runs on your machine; your history never leaves it. Built with Rust, Tauri and Svelte.

[Website](https://lumeagents.dev) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Download](https://github.com/Lume-agents/Lume/releases/latest) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Source](https://github.com/Lume-agents/Lume) &nbsp;·&nbsp; ![latest release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/Lume-agents/Lume?label=latest&style=flat-square&color=5f8a6a&labelColor=18231b)
